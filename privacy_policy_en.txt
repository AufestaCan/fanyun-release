FanYun Keyboard Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2026
Effective: October 2026

This application ("the App") is developed and operated by Shenzhen Futian
District Fanyun Information Technology Studio ("we"). The App follows an
"offline-first" design: everything you type is processed locally on your
device and never leaves it.

Please read this policy carefully before use. Tapping "Agree" means you
understand and accept the policy in full; if you do not agree, tap
"Decline & exit" and the App will not make any network requests.

1. How we collect and use information

1.1 Typed content
    Everything you type with the keyboard — letters, words, symbols and
    the like — is processed only on your device, for candidate
    computation and committing text. We never upload, store or analyse
    anything you type.

1.2 Data stored locally
    The following data stays inside the App's private directory and is
    never synced to any server:
    - Usage-frequency learning data (for the local "more used, higher
      ranked" candidate ordering)
    - Your custom quick phrases
    - Appearance, layout and sound settings
    - Cached dictionary files, exchange-rate data and promotion
      configuration

1.3 Clipboard
    The App reads the current system clipboard only when you actively
    tap the keyboard's "paste" key, to insert that content. The App does
    not record, store or upload clipboard history.

1.4 Device information
    In normal operation the App does not collect device identifiers,
    model or system information.

2. Network activity

The App declares the network permission solely for the following
"read-only download" behaviours. No network request is made without
your consent, and your typed content is never uploaded at any time:

2.1 Version and dictionary checks: the App requests a configuration
    file from the update repository (gitee.com) to check for app and
    dictionary updates. The request carries only three dimensions —
    platform (android), build channel, and the system region code (e.g.
    CN) — and no personal identifier.
2.2 Dictionary updates: downloading updated dictionary data files
    (pure data, no executable code).
2.3 Promotional content: receiving self-operated promotion text or
    image assets, always labelled "Ads" before display. They can be
    turned off permanently under Settings → Appearance → Ads, after
    which no related configuration is received.
2.4 Exchange rates: only when you use the currency-conversion feature
    on the keyboard and the local cache is older than 6 hours, the App
    requests current rates from public rate sources (cdn.jsdelivr.net,
    frankfurter.dev).
2.5 Share image: when you open "Recommend", the App downloads the
    referral QR image.

3. Sharing, transfer and disclosure

The App embeds no third-party SDK, analytics or ad-tracking service,
and does not share, transfer or publicly disclose your information to
any third party.

4. Data storage and deletion

All of the App's data is stored locally on your device. You can delete
all of it at any time via the system settings' "clear app data", or by
uninstalling the App — this completely removes the local data
(including learning data, quick phrases and all settings).

5. Permissions

- Internet access (INTERNET): for the read-only downloads listed in
  section 2.
- Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check connectivity.
- High-rate sensor sampling (HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS): a leftover
  declaration from a discontinued experiment. No feature calls it in
  this version; it will be removed in a later release.

6. Feedback (debug builds)

Internal test builds offer an optional feedback entry. After your
explicit confirmation in the dialog, it uploads a screenshot of the
keyboard area, a redacted action log (no typed text), and device
model/OS/app version for troubleshooting. The screenshot captures the
keyboard's own area only. Release builds do not include this feature.

7. Minors

The App does not actively collect any personal information and does
not process information differently by age. Minors should use the App
under guardian guidance.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features change; the latest version
shown in the App prevails.
